## Placeholder

Baseline rules every Kronk instance is encouraged to keep:

1. No harassment. Disagreement is welcome; personal targeting isn't.
2. No illegal content.
3. Content warnings for anything a reasonable person would want a
   heads-up about.
4. No CSAM or other non-consensual sexual material.

Operators should extend these with anything specific to their community.
