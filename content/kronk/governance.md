## How Kronk-the-project is governed

Kronk uses a **Kommons** pattern — every structural decision routes
through a public proposal.

### Proposals — the coordination surface

Anyone can plant a proposal in Kommons: here's an idea, here's what
it might look like, here's what's needed to build it. Proposals
hang off the node they concern — a korner, a page, a shared
vocabulary. The Kommons Directory maps every real page in Kronk to
the proposals about it, so feedback lives _within_ the surface it
concerns rather than in a separate forum.

Simple proposals gather the people who want to help build a thing.
Structural proposals — visibility scopes, storage discipline,
reserved vocabulary, the manifest schema, the feed contract — need
demonstrated support (backing) before they enact.

## Stewards and maintainers

The people credited on [Contributors](/kronk/contributors) built
and maintain each korner's code. Steward is **recognition, not
power** — being named a steward doesn't grant runtime moderation
authority beyond what any account has.

Runtime moderation, when it's needed, escalates to the instance
admins through the standard Mastodon report flow. Structural
moderation (deprecating a korner, resetting a shared scope) routes
through Kommons.
