## How Kronk-the-project is governed

Kronk uses a **Seed / Kommons** pattern.

### Seeds — plant an idea

Anyone can plant a Seed. A Seed is a lightweight coordination surface:
here's an idea, here's what it might look like, here's what's needed
to build it. No approval required — the point of a Seed is to gather
the people who want to work on something.

### Kommons — structural moves

Anything that touches the platform's spine — visibility scopes,
storage discipline, reserved vocabulary, the manifest schema, the
feed contract — is a Kommons proposal. Kommons proposals require
support to enact.

Sub-layers are places; ideas are things to build. Don't route a Seed
through Kommons; don't try to plant a scope change as a Seed.

## Stewards and maintainers

The people credited on `/kronk/contributors` are the ones who built
and maintain each korner's code. Steward is **recognition, not power**:
being named a steward doesn't grant runtime moderation authority
beyond what any account has.

Runtime moderation, when it's needed, escalates to the instance admins
through the standard Mastodon report flow. Structural moderation
(deprecating a korner, resetting a shared scope) routes through Kommons.
