## Kronk 2.0 rebuild

The rebuild is underway on the `rebuild/2.0.0` branch. Everything Kronk has learned since the 1.x line — the korner framework, the Nudges paradigm, the Hub landing, the org space you're reading right now — folds into it.

**What's new so far:**

- A **Hub landing page** listing every korner
- **Tune-in / tune-out** — every korner can be muted from your feed with one click
- **Nudges Activity** — aggregated chat-form reactions to your posts ("3 froths on your post" instead of three bell dings)
- **Multi-group posting** — a post can address several groups at once
- **Sectioned profile** — arrange your `@you` view around korners and kategories, not just a timeline
- **/kronk/** org space — this thing you're reading, drawn from markdown files in the repo

**Currently on shadow only:** the rebuild ships at [shadow.kronk.info](https://shadow.kronk.info). `mastodon.kronk.info` stays on 1.7.x until the branch merges.
