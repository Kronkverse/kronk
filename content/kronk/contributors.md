## How to become a contributor

The bar is not "have you shipped in production before"; the bar is
"do you care about this and are you willing to iterate with the
rest of us."

Concrete path:

1. Read [How it works](/kronk/how-it-works) and, if you're
   building a korner, `docs/korners/adding_a_korner.md` +
   `docs/korners/korner_standard.md` in the repo.
2. Plant a proposal in Kommons — pick a page (a korner, a shared
   surface) and hang your idea on it. Anyone can back it.
3. Everything upstream from the discussion lives in the repo:
   [github.com/Kronkverse/kronk](https://github.com/Kronkverse/kronk).
