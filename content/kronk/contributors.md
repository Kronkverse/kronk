## How to become a contributor

Read `docs/korners/adding_a_korner.md`, plant a Seed for what you want
to build, and open the discussion on Kommons. Everything upstream from
that lives in the repo:

- <https://github.com/Kronkverse/kronk>

The bar is not "have you shipped in production before"; the bar is
"do you care about this and are you willing to iterate with the rest
of us."
