    Our vision

    “Bringing a dream into waking reality is the key to setting it free. Once the door is open, together we can walk through into the great unknown of our dream reality.”
    Read the vision 5 points
    • Build a system where people are rewarded for following their passions and for making the world a better place: heART.
    • A community that supports people stepping into their passion/s, and grows into the potential of being paid to follow their art.
    • e.g. Release a song, everyone gives $2 to back the artist pushing the edge: 100 people × $2 = $200 for that release.
    • None of it is a ladder. Healing feeds art, art feeds community, community feeds healing. The web shows how it all flows.
    • It's a space for all dreams to make the web, so there's a strong foundation for all our dreams to be set free.
